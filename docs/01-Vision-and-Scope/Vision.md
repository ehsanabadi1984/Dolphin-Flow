# 1. Purpose

This document defines the vision, strategic objectives, and long-term direction of Dolphin Flow.

It explains why the platform is being created and what value it is expected to provide to the organization.


# 2. Product Vision

Dolphin Flow aims to become the digital operational backbone of the organization by providing a unified platform for managing, automating, and improving business processes.

The platform transforms fragmented manual workflows into structured, measurable, and transparent digital processes.


# 3. Mission Statement

The mission of Dolphin Flow is:

"To simplify organizational operations by connecting people, processes, information, and decisions in a controlled digital environment."


# 4. Current Business Challenges

Before Dolphin Flow, organizational processes may depend on:

- Paper-based forms
- Manual approvals
- Phone-based follow-ups
- Individual experience
- Separate department records
- Limited process visibility


These approaches create challenges such as:

- Process delays
- Information loss
- Difficulty tracking responsibilities
- Lack of performance measurement
- Limited historical analysis


# 5. Solution Approach

Dolphin Flow addresses these challenges by providing:


## Process Automation

Business processes are executed through predefined workflows.


## Transparency

Every process step has:

- Owner
- Status
- Timeline
- History


## Accountability

Every action is linked to a responsible user or department.


## Measurement

Process performance is measured using:

- SLA
- Reports
- Dashboards


## Knowledge Preservation

Organizational knowledge is stored inside the system instead of depending only on individuals.


# 6. Strategic Goals


## Short-Term Goals

Implement a reliable digital repair workflow system.

Expected outcomes:

- Eliminate manual repair forms
- Track every device lifecycle
- Reduce repair delays
- Improve customer communication
- Increase operational visibility


## Medium-Term Goals

Expand Dolphin Flow into additional organizational workflows.

Examples:

- Quality processes
- Internal requests
- Service operations
- Administrative workflows


## Long-Term Goals

Establish Dolphin Flow as the organization's workflow and operational management platform.


# 7. Product Principles


## Principle 1: Workflow First

Business logic should be represented as workflows instead of being embedded directly in code.


## Principle 2: Audit Everything Important

Important actions must be traceable.


## Principle 3: Human-Centered Design

The system must be designed around user experience and operational efficiency.


## Principle 4: Flexible Architecture

The platform must support future expansion without major redesign.


## Principle 5: Security by Design

Access control, permissions, and data protection are core requirements.


# 8. Success Criteria


Dolphin Flow will be considered successful when:


## Operational Success

- Departments use the system as the primary process management tool.
- Manual tracking is minimized.


## Process Success

- Every process has a clear owner.
- Every stage is measurable.
- Delays are visible.


## Data Success

- Complete historical records are available.
- Reports can be generated from operational data.


## User Success

- Users can complete their tasks quickly and correctly.
- The interface reduces complexity instead of increasing it.


# 9. Future Direction

Dolphin Flow is designed as a platform, not a single-purpose application.

Future capabilities may include:

- Customer portal
- Mobile applications
- AI-assisted operations
- Predictive analytics
- Advanced workflow designer
- Integration with ERP and external systems


# 10. Current Scope Focus

The first implementation focuses on:

Repair and After-Sales Service Workflow


This process will validate the platform foundation before expanding into other business areas.
